How to read billing efficiencyDefinitions, workbook measures, and follow-up guidance

Selected month summary

The month selector changes the summary cards and the state, zone, representative, product, and zero-billing details.

Billing efficiency

Efficiency is billed active distributor codes divided by active distributor codes. A billed distributor has positive Value In Lacs for that month.

Monthly trend

Each row shows one workbook month. Compare sales and distributor coverage together; a larger sales total does not always mean more distributors billed.

State, zone, and representative

Sales and coverage are grouped using the labels in the billing workbook. Representative assignment uses its Res SR/SO Name field.

Product billing coverage

Coverage counts active distributors with a positive value in each product column. Product amounts are omitted because the workbook does not define their unit.

Active distributors without positive billing

This follow-up list includes active codes with zero or negative net billing in the selected month. Confirm the workbook row and current account status before acting.
